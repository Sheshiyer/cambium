resolve, inspected source hashes match, owner/evidence distinctions remain accurate,
interview answers are recorded, and another client has a concrete next read-only
step. A broken source identity, incorrect writer assignment, unqualified live claim,
lost existing work or missing handoff fails the checkpoint.

phased build plan and unresolved choices recorded. Native implementation, signed-app

explicit next-stage deliverables. Closing discovery must not claim those happened
or that the broader build horizon is complete. Routing/Cato limitations remain
