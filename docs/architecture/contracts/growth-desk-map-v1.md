# Growth desk source map v1

This is a curated public topology projection of the private growth department.
The six desks sit inside Will. They are roles, not six new agents or daemons.
Document ownership, principal organ, organ inputs and intended execution bindings
are different fields. No row proves a running worker or grants delivery authority.

| Desk | Document owner | Principal organ | Will desk | Intended execution binding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Head of Marketing | CEO | Genesis | strategy | CEO (approve) + Synthesist (brief) |
| Copywriter | CEO | Hands | dispatch | Synthesist (primary writer) |
| Creative Strategist | CEO | Taste | interpretation | Designer + Synthesist |
| Launch Lead | CEO | Will | dispatch | CEO + Hermes (cards only) |
| SEO Lead | CEO | Hands | retrieval | Scientist + Engineer |
| Analyst | CEO | Cortex | audit | Scientist |

The execution-map source explicitly describes these bindings as a **goal** that
does not deploy anything; its distribution skill is **candidate**. They remain
source contracts. A future runtime assignment requires its own scoped identity,
loadout, admission, callback, artifact, verifier and receipt.

Principal connections in the reviewed [ecosystem topology](growth-ecosystem-topology-v1.md)
are complementary: positioning, grading, business interpretation and audit
responsibilities do not replace a desk's principal organ or its intended runner.
Copy uses Genesis copy plus Taste and passes through Hands. Creative uses Taste
to guide Hands. Launch uses the Will spine; SEO uses Hands Search Stack; Analyst
uses Taste and Cortex. Every desk's business interpretation remains inside Will.

## Source ownership

The owning private records are `20-operations/growth/department/execution-map.md`
(`## Krebs org ↔ department desks`), `department/organ-map.md`
(`## Will desks (C-suite inside the organ)`) and the six desk READMEs. Their
frontmatter declares `owner: ceo`, the principal `organ` and `will_desk` shown
above. Private bodies, source inventories and proof receipts stay in the vault.
This projection publishes only generic role and organ topology.

Extract, feed, read and edit keep their separate effects. A draft, quality score
or role binding grants no publication, campaign enrollment or recurring cadence.
