# Marketing capability to asset orchestration

Status: synthetic, offline, review-only foundation.

## Outcome

Cambium can turn a founder-selected product packet, asset outcome, objective, and channel outcome into a
deterministic marketing capability plan and review package. The founder never selects a skill or recipe. A
closed outcome resolver chooses the one matching recipe, and the plan chooses the
smallest curated chain, binds its upstream provenance, and leaves every artifact awaiting human approval.

This slice does not publish, send, schedule, spend, authenticate, call a provider, deploy, or mutate an
external system. A zero-dollar external action is still an action and remains behind the same authority gates.

    product truth + evidence snapshot
      -> outcome-first recipe
      -> pinned capability plan
      -> asset brief
      -> canonical draft
      -> quality report
      -> review-only channel package
      -> [blocked: canonical approval not wired] content_asset@1.0.0
      -> exact approval + lease + fencing before any future engage action

## Organic and low-budget media

The useful zero-spend precursor always exists before a paid render:

- Text channels produce a brief, canonical draft, variants, claim ledger, quality report, and accessibility
  companion using local deterministic orchestration.
- Image and video capabilities produce a prompt pack, storyboard, aspect-ratio matrix, alt text or transcript,
  rights checklist, and provider-neutral production specification.
- A separate low-budget quote may record currency, maximum per-run amount, billing unit, and quote expiry,
  while the canonical runtime spend policy remains spend:none and runtime_enabled remains false. It cannot
  bind a credential or adapter and cannot reserve or spend money.
- Free publication or community posting is not classified as spend none; it is an external write and remains
  forbidden until an exact action is approved and leased.

This preserves a clean future seam for a local model, subscription tool, ElevenLabs, Runway, Composio, or
another renderer. Every future renderer needs its own provider contract, typed secret reference, budget
reservation, rights policy, deterministic fixture, kill switch, approval, and reconciliation tranche.

## Deterministic proof

The proof validates the pinned catalog and six internal contracts; compiles every recipe; refuses draft
execution for compiled-only recipes and loop execution for compiled-only loops; accepts
a synthetic product packet and canonically digested verified evidence snapshot; rejects recipe/channel drift;
injects clock and seed; canonicalizes collections
before hashing; compiles the ordered capability plan; produces a review-only founder article package; emits a
redacted exact-digest receipt; and replays identically while the network sentinel records zero calls.

Rights, freshness, package integrity, and source lineage are recomputed only to explain bridge blockers. The
bridge remains closed with canonical_approval_decision_required, always keeps publish eligibility false, and
requires the separate suppression, action-request, approval, writer-lease, and fencing gates in Engage.
Identical historical bytes cannot make a capability-plane package authoritative.

The local founder surface is explicit and deterministic:

    node bin/marketing-orchestration.mjs validate
    node bin/marketing-orchestration.mjs plan --outcome social_draft_set --now 2026-07-18T12:00:00.000Z --seed social-plan-v1
    node bin/marketing-orchestration.mjs proof --now 2026-07-18T12:00:00.000Z --seed founder-review-v1
    node bin/marketing-orchestration.mjs loop-plan --loop-id content-repurposing@1.0.0
    node bin/marketing-orchestration.mjs loop --now 2026-07-18T12:00:00.000Z --seed manual-check-v1

The CLI returns a redacted package projection and receipt, never the draft body. Clock and seed are mandatory
for proof and loop commands; there is no hidden wall-clock or random fallback.

## Operator projection and accessibility

A Hermes projection may expose recipe or loop ID, truthful state, artifact count, package digest, bounded
reason and recovery codes, next action, and replay truth. It must not expose draft bodies, lead identity,
provider payloads, secret references, raw evidence, or private product context.

Every supported medium declares an accessibility companion: alt text for images, transcripts/captions for
audio or video, semantic headings for long-form text, and plain-language channel notes. Review states use
text and machine-readable codes, never color alone. A blocked or skipped item states why and how to recover.
