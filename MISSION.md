# Cambium Mission

## Authority and lifecycle

This file owns Cambium's singular Repository Mission: a renewable doctrine horizon that translates the enduring Vision into a bounded current pursuit. It is doctrine, not a goal ledger, task queue, runtime projection, or second planning authority.

[`ISA.md`](./ISA.md) owns approved goals and acceptance. GSD, rooted in [`.planning/`](./.planning/), owns finite planning state. Renewal changes this Mission only through explicit review of the evidence and boundaries below.

## Current pursuit

Establish a provenance-preserving infinite-game architecture in which canonical doctrine, finite goals, planning, evidence, and learning stay connected without copying authority or erasing historical context.

## Horizon

The current event-bounded horizon ends with reviewed verification and release completion of milestone v0.4. A calendar date may schedule a review, but time alone cannot close, renew, or replace the Repository Mission.

## Evidence of progress

Progress is demonstrated by checked requirements, completed plan summaries, verification results, and a reviewed handoff that agree on the same bounded repository state. Metrics and generated views may support that evidence; they do not become doctrine or completion authority by themselves.

## Renewal triggers

Review this Mission when the v0.4 horizon closes, when verified evidence shows that the pursuit no longer advances the Vision, when viability or authority boundaries materially change, or when the current pursuit has become too broad to remain falsifiable.

A review may renew the pursuit with a new bounded horizon, narrow it, or propose a successor. Renewal must record its evidence, decision, and relationship to the unchanged Vision.

## Retirement or replacement

Retire this Mission explicitly when its pursuit is complete or no longer serves the Vision. Replace it explicitly when a different bounded pursuit is approved. Retirement or replacement requires reviewed evidence and a recorded handoff; silence, elapsed time, feature completion, or a generated status cannot perform either transition.

## Inherited boundaries

Finite goals inherit direction from the Repository Mission while retaining ISA acceptance and GSD planning authority. Generated, operational, and connected surfaces may reference these anchors but cannot rewrite them.

No `FabricMission` inherits or rewrites repository doctrine. A `FabricMission` remains a bounded child record inside one `WorkObject`, with operational state owned by the D1 Goal Graph and projected read-only by Mission Fabric.
