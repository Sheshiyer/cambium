# Visual Guidelines

## Design Philosophy
Precision meets regulatory authority. Clear validation signals over decoration.

## Color System
- Primary: #2EE600
- Secondary: #1C1917
- Accent: #FAFAF9

## Typography
- Header Font: Geist Sans
- Body Font: Geist Sans

## Logo Usage
Use the canonical IVerif mark; never stretch or recolor off-palette.

## Composition
- Precision instrument panel
- Clear validation signals

## Anti-Patterns
- Generic SaaS gradients
- Stock handshake photography
