# Proposed Cambium native stories

Research backlog, 2 November 2026. No stories are implemented or accepted by
this document. Root `ISA.md` is the acceptance ledger. These check descriptions
are proposed probes to promote into stable ISA IDs after scope discussion.

The useful first milestone is one read-only whole-system → selected work →
evidence → next permitted step, with persistence limited to local preferences.
It is an intermediate milestone. The founder-selected first usable release
also coordinates decisions and launches already-admitted work, observes results
and recovers uncertain outcomes. A passive viewer does not satisfy v1.

Native authentication remains unproved. It blocks all Gate-dependent stories
and the real authenticated portion of read stories. Fixture-only work must be
labelled and cannot satisfy live acceptance. Story checks are proposed probes,
not a second acceptance ledger.

## M0 — settle the native contract

### S-001 Explain the app's purpose
As the founder, I open the brief and recognize the whole-system-first job.
Screens: N01, N02. Status: proposed.
Checks: `[manual]` founder reviews the brief; `[source]` every platform/owner
assumption is recorded with a source or pending probe. Discovery: ISC-3140–3147.

### S-002 Choose an implementation baseline
As a maintainer, I select a deployment target, app identity and renderer from
measured options. Depends: S-001. Status: proposed.
Checks: `[build]` minimal fixture app compiles on the chosen target;
`[manual]` same orientation task is compared across bounded renderer options;
`[source]` existing app/install identity remains preserved. Discovery: ISC-3164.

## M1 — orient, focus, understand

### S-003 Open the Canopy
As the founder, I see the system's purpose, work and attention without reading
every node. Screens: N01. Depends: S-002. Status: proposed.
Checks: `[snap]` labelled source-only topology in light/dark;
`[self]` each work item is reachable by keyboard/search;
`[unit]` source adjacency cannot create operational status.

### S-004 Focus one WorkObject
As the founder, I select work and see its exact identity, objective and gaps.
Screens: N02. Depends: S-003. Status: proposed.
Checks: `[unit]` exact-ID joins and ambiguity refusal;
`[snap]` missing metadata stays explicit;
`[self]` back/lens changes preserve selection.

### S-005 Inspect one evidence chain
As the founder, I follow a task to its attempt, artifact, receipt and verdict.
Screens: N03, N04. Depends: S-004. Status: proposed.
Checks: `[unit]` wrong subject/tenant/digest rejected;
`[snap]` absent consumer or verdict remains a gap;
`[self]` source opens through its owning surface.

### S-006 Recover from an unavailable read
As the founder, I can distinguish offline, denied, empty and stale information.
Screens: N01–N04, N08. Depends: S-005. Status: proposed.
Checks: `[unit]` stale responses cannot overwrite newer reads;
`[self]` network loss retains labelled context but removes current action eligibility;
`[unit]` tenant/principal switch invalidates incompatible cache.

### S-007 See the next meaningful step
As the founder, I see a reasoned next read, evidence request or review candidate.
Screens: N02, N04. Depends: S-006. Status: proposed.
Checks: `[unit]` no candidate becomes approval/admission;
`[manual]` founder identifies the next step and its reason in a realistic session;
`[snap]` a hold states the missing evidence and owning source.

## M2 — connect the knowledge and runtime lenses

As the founder, I choose one saved source/node and understand its relevance to

`[self]` missing CLI and stale node cases;

As the founder, I inspect relevant citations without merging memory planes.

## M3 — deliberate actions and durable outcomes

### S-011 Review the complete current proposal
As an authorized founder, I inspect the full server-owned change before choosing.
Screens: N05. Depends: S-006, native identity probe. Status: proposed.
Checks: `[unit]` complete review, expected head, version, digest, nonce and expiry;
`[self]` identity/tenant refusal and changed-review cases;
`[manual]` exact consequential decision remains deliberate.

### S-012 Inspect an uncertain outcome
As the founder, I reopen the original decision tuple after losing a response.
Screens: N06. Depends: S-011. Status: proposed.
Checks: `[unit]` status-only recovery performs zero extra decision submissions;
`[snap]` decision, connection and execution are independent;
`[self]` relaunch retains the original reference without storing credentials.

### S-013 Observe accepted foldback
As the founder, I distinguish artifact delivery, independent acceptance and
learning proposal. Screens: N03, N04. Depends: S-005. Status: proposed.
Checks: `[contract]` owning producer/consumer/verdict joins;
`[snap]` source support versus unavailable live acceptance;
`[unit]` no learning action silently rewrites D1 or routing policy.

### S-017 Launch an already-admitted work unit
As the founder, I inspect the current admission, loadout and execution plant,
then deliberately launch that exact supported work unit. Screens: N02, N11.
Depends: S-011, native identity and owning launch capability. Status: proposed.
Checks: `[contract]` owner atomically revalidates admission/revision/pins/scope;
`[unit]` a candidate, expired admission or changed input cannot launch;
`[self]` one request identity yields one durable launch result or explicit unknown;
`[manual]` one authorized real work class launches from the actual native app.

### S-018 Follow a run to its result
As the founder, I see the owning plant's queued/claimed/running/terminal state
and inspect its artifact and independent verdict. Screens: N11, N04.
Depends: S-017. Status: proposed.
Checks: `[contract]` task/request/attempt/receipt binding;
`[snap]` connection and execution do not imply acceptance;
`[self]` terminal failure and missing verdict remain inspectable.

### S-019 Recover an uncertain launch after restart
As the founder, I reopen work after a lost response or app restart and reconcile
the original launch before attempting another. Screens: N11, N06.
Depends: S-017, S-018. Status: proposed.
Checks: `[unit]` timeout/relaunch/status recovery creates zero duplicate dispatches;
`[contract]` busy/terminal/expired/unknown owner results are distinct;
`[self]` recovery references persist without credentials or raw job bodies.

### S-020 Choose a supported execution plant
As the founder, I can distinguish local Hands from remote Hermes and launch only
where the current owner capability supports the admitted work. Screens: N08, N11.
Depends: S-009, S-017. Status: proposed.
Checks: `[contract]` plant and work capability are exact, not inferred from name;
`[snap]` unsupported class explains the owning next step;
`[unit]` native client cannot claim a Hermes lease or emit executor attestations.

## M4 — native craft and distribution

### S-014 Operate by keyboard and assistive technology
As the user, I complete orientation, exact decision confirmation, admitted launch,
result inspection and uncertain-outcome recovery without spatial navigation.
Screens: N01–N09 and N11. Depends: M1; action/run coverage follows S-017–S-020. Status: proposed.
Checks: `[self]` command/menu parity and focus across orientation and action/run states;
`[manual]` VoiceOver, reduced motion, text size and contrast through launch/recovery;
`[snap]` every panel and state in light/dark.

### S-015 Use a small entry point
As the user, I open the selected next step through an explicitly chosen menu bar,
shortcut or deep link. Depends: M1 and entry-point decision. Status: proposed.
Checks: `[self]` opens exact work with no automatic mutation;
`[unit]` hostile/unsupported URL refused;
`[manual]` entry point adds daily value before enabling notifications.

### S-016 Install and recover a native release
As the user, I launch an identified native artifact and can recover safely.
Depends: selected distribution scope. Status: proposed.
Checks: `[build]` exact source/artifact/signature/notarization evidence;
`[manual]` real Mac install, auth expiry, offline restart and upgrade/recovery;
`[source]` Electron/Curious retirement is a separate decision.

## First-release completion boundary

The first usable release includes S-003–S-007, S-011–S-014 and S-016–S-020 for
the selected supported work/plant scope. Knowledge breadth S-008/S-010 and small
entry points S-015 can expand progressively, but required data sources remain
visible with honest supported/unavailable state. The release needs actual native
orientation, decision, admitted launch, outcome and recovery evidence; local
fixture success alone cannot close it. Story order is dependency-based, not ID order.

## Deliberately uncommitted scope

External-user distribution, team authoring, mobile-native apps, full native 3D,
automatic schedules, full agent terminal hosting, secrets sync, machine migration,
provider configuration, direct publication and automatic learning promotion need
their own product decisions. These are not silently dropped from the long vision.
