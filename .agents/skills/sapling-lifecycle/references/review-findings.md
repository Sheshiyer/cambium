## Authority and duplication

Canonical vault sources remain in the vault. The catalog stores only public-safe interface descriptions, provenance and bounded findings. Reports may read allowlisted source material when supplied, but neither startup discovery nor installed commands dump a vault corpus into context.

The new deterministic helpers prepare reports and one-step briefs. They do not claim native model behavior from file-existence tests, or production acceptance from mocked tests. Discovery, local behavior tests, semantic workflow completion and authenticated external readback need distinct evidence.
