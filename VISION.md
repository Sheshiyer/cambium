# Cambium Vision

## Just Cause

Cambium's singular, near-invariant Just Cause is continued meaningful play through coordinated finite games: a fractal tapestry in which ventures, programs, people, agents, and tools can keep learning, adapting, and contributing without collapsing into one final product or one permanent center.

## Infinite-game commitments

- Keep the conditions for meaningful participation, learning, and renewal alive.
- Coordinate finite games as bounded expressions of the larger cause, each with honest authority, evidence, and stopping conditions.
- Let verified evidence inform slower layers without allowing a temporary metric or local win to silently rewrite doctrine.
- Preserve plurality: the tapestry remains coherent because its parts share boundaries and provenance, not because every part becomes the same product.

## Non-goals

Revenue, growth, feature completion, and a single product endpoint are not the finish line. They may provide fuel, evidence, or finite-game outcomes, but none defines completion of Cambium's infinite game.

This Vision is not an issue queue, task list, release plan, runtime contract, or claim that perpetual operation excuses an unbounded finite activity.

## Authority and change

Only an explicit doctrine review changes the Vision. Ordinary delivery evidence, milestone completion, market movement, or generated projections may trigger that review but cannot change the Vision automatically.

[`ISA.md`](./ISA.md) owns approved goals and acceptance. GSD, rooted in [`.planning/`](./.planning/), owns finite planning state. These authorities inherit direction from the doctrine anchors; neither anchor becomes a competing planner.
