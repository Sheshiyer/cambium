# Future Milestone Context: D1 Admission and Hermes EC2 Activation

**Status:** Draft planning input only; not an active GSD milestone
**Prerequisite:** v0.5 Phase 9 read-only source inventory must be independently verified
**Source:** retained D1/Hermes EC2 draft from the reviewed Cambium handoff

## Intent

Prepare one provenance-bound D1 admission and Hermes EC2 activation path that
could later prove exactly one rollback-bounded runner canary. Keep the path
finite, receipt-backed, and fail-closed at every owner-controlled boundary.

## Ordering with v0.5

v0.5 remains the active milestone. Its Phase 9 must first produce exact,
authenticated, read-only `9d9d` and `thoughtseed-labs` inventory evidence.
v0.5 Phase 10 allowlisted reconciliation and 9d9d retirement remain a
separate approval-gated decision; this context does not merge those operations
with D1 or Hermes work.

When a new milestone is legitimately opened, this context can become the
basis for global phases 11–13 (or the next available GSD phase numbers):

1. **Compatibility and admission preflight** — inspect Hermes Desktop/EC2
   compatibility, source dirtiness, service ownership, rollback requirements,
   and one exact D1 candidate packet without writing or executing anything.
2. **Transport and dry-run proof** — verify approved Telegram and authenticated
   bridge prerequisites and read pending delivery intents while execution
   flags remain disabled.
3. **Single canary gate** — only after separate owner approval, run one bounded
   canary and capture terminal outcome, ACK, foldback, inbound receipt, and
   rollback evidence.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- The D1 Goal Graph remains the operational writer; Hermes remains transport
  and execution supervisor, not task authority.
- No EC2 update, stash, backup, source rewrite, package install, service
  restart, Desktop reconnection, or Remote Gateway experiment is implied.
- No Telegram token write, gateway restart, outbound message, D1/KV/R2 write,
  Worker deploy, DNS, Access, tunnel, or directive execution is implied.
- The existing EC2 source tree remains user-modified and must be inspected
  without overwriting it.
- `HERMES_RUNNER_EXECUTE_DIRECTIVES=false` and
  `HERMES_RUNNER_LEGACY_ACK_WITHOUT_EXECUTION=false` remain preserved until
  the relevant approvals are explicitly recorded.

## Admission decision required

Before executable admission planning, the owner must select one exact
WorkObject candidate and provide its canonical identity and kind, mapping
receipt/readback, eligible task or directive, expiry, rollback reference, and
terminal verification receipt shape. No candidate is selected by this draft.

## Handoff from Phase 9

Phase 9's inventory packet is evidence for planning continuity only. It cannot
be treated as D1 admission, Hermes compatibility, Telegram transport, or
runner readiness proof. Each later phase must obtain its own tool-verified
receipt and stop independently on missing authority or stale state.
