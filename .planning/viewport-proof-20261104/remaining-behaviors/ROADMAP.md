# Finite viewport proposal roadmap

Goal: a user can reach and inspect every original retained mission, proof, story, tool, policy, skill, actor, ActionRequest and portfolio behavior in the maintained 3D world, with exact source identity and honest state. After separately admitted source implementation, all 43 retained intents have complete current local-browser equivalents; no live/device/owner/release authority follows.

Status: proposed. This roadmap is the bounded breakdown referenced by ISA, not a replacement for root STATE/ROADMAP or its active Phase 9. Requirements below are references to the unchecked ISA proposal; ISA remains their authority.
