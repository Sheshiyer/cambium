- `compileDocumentationInventory` returns one validated object with full `sourceRevision`, sorted entry identity, `sourceSetDigest`, and `inventoryDigest`; `renderDocumentationInventoryJson` and `renderDocumentationInventoryMarkdown` are pure views of that same object.
